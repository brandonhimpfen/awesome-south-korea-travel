## Official & Planning

Essential official government entry portals, tourism boards, electronic travel authorizations, and weather tracking.

- [VISITKOREA (Korea Tourism Organization)](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/) - Official national tourism portal featuring destination highlights, regional itineraries, and cultural event calendars.
- [K-ETA Official Portal](https://www.k-eta.go.kr/) - Official electronic travel authorization system required for eligible visa-free visitors entering South Korea.
- [Visit Seoul (Official City Portal)](https://english.visitseoul.net/) - Official municipal guide for exploring Seoul's neighborhoods, historical palaces, and cultural attractions.
- [Korea Meteorological Administration (KMA)](https://www.weather.go.kr/index.ng) - Official national weather service providing forecasts, typhoon tracking, and seasonal climate reports.

## Transportation & Rail

Navigating high-speed KTX bullet trains, metro systems, domestic aviation, and transit cards.

- [Let's Korail (Korea Railroad Corporation)](https://www.letskorail.com/) - Official national railway operator managing KTX high-speed lines and intercity train connections across South Korea.
- [Seoul Metro](https://www.seoulmetro.co.kr/en/) - Official operator guiding rapid transit lines, station amenities, and subway navigation in the capital region.
- [Korean Air](https://www.koreanair.com/) - National flag carrier operating domestic routes and international long-haul flights through Incheon International Airport (ICN).
- [Asiana Airlines](https://flyasiana.com/) - Major domestic and international carrier connecting regional hubs like Gimpo (GMP) and Jeju (CJU).

## Destinations & Regions

### Seoul & Capital Area

- [Seoul Metropolitan Government Tourism Info](https://english.visitseoul.net/) - Comprehensive municipal portal detailing royal palaces (Gyeongbokgung), Bukchon Hanok Village, and shopping districts.
- [HiKR GROUND (Korea Tourism Organization)](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/) - Interactive K-culture and K-pop exhibition center located in central Seoul.

### Busan & Southeastern Coast

- [Visit Busan (Official City Portal)](https://www.visitbusan.net/) - Official municipal visitor guide for Haeundae Beach, Gamcheon Culture Village, and coastal seafood markets.

### Jeju Island

- [Visit Jeju (Jeju Tourism Organization)](https://www.visitjeju.net/) - Official regional portal for exploring Hallasan volcanic trails, coastal lava tubes, and tangerine farms.

### Gyeongju & Historic Regions

- [Gyeongju City Tourism Portal](https://www.gyeongju.go.kr/) - Official travel hub for exploring Silla Dynasty royal tombs, Bulguksa Temple, and Gyeongju National Museum.

## Navigation & Connectivity

Mapping tools, public Wi-Fi access, and digital transit integration.

- [Naver Map (English Web Version)](https://map.naver.com/) - Essential mapping platform optimized for accurate public transit routing, walking directions, and local reviews in South Korea.
- [KakaoMap](https://map.kakao.com/) - Popular navigation app providing real-time bus locations, driving directions, and localized point-of-interest search.

## Safety & Emergency Services

Practical advice on 24-hour tourist helplines, emergency contact numbers, and medical assistance.

- [1330 Korea Travel Helpline (KTO)](https://english.visitkorea.or.kr/) - 24-hour multi-language helpline offering tourist assistance, interpretation services, and emergency support (Dial **1330**).
- [Emergency Numbers (Police 112 / Fire & Ambulance 119)](https://www.gov.kr/) - Direct national emergency dispatch numbers operational across all provinces.
